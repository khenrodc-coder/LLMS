Laundry Lounge is a full-stack management platform built for real-time operations — from machine monitoring to customer tracking, all in one place.

Roles: Super Admin Admin Operator Customer
01 — Machine Status
Live Machine Monitoring

All 8 washing machines tracked in real time — free, busy, idle, or under maintenance. Auto-refreshes every 10 seconds with per-unit progress timers.

Super AdminOperator
02 — Analytics
Analytics Dashboard

Daily and monthly revenue line charts, weekly bar graphs, service mix pie charts, and staff performance bars — all live-updating across all portals.

Super AdminAdmin
03 — Encoding
Service Encoding

Register walk-in or customer services with live price calculation, machine assignment, promo code validation, and optional dryer and downy add-ons.

AdminOperator
04 — Tracking
Real-Time Laundry Status

Customers see live stage progress — Pending, Washing, Drying, Downy, Folding, Ready for Pickup — with live countdown timers and notifications.

CustomerOperator
05 — Staff
Staff Management

Create, edit, activate, deactivate, or archive staff accounts. View 30-day performance metrics including total services handled and revenue generated.

AdminSuper Admin
06 — Emergency
Emergency Controls

Instantly trigger system shutdown, restore operations, force logout all sessions, bulk-reset passwords, or disable all promo codes — all logged to audit trail.

Super Admin
07 — Promos
Promo Code System

Admins create percentage-based discount codes. Operators validate and apply them live during service encoding. Customers see discounts on tracking and receipts.

AdminOperatorCustomer
08 — Feedback
Customer Feedback

Customers rate completed orders with 1–5 stars and optional comments after pickup. Admins see aggregate ratings, averages, 5-star counts, and individual reviews.

CustomerAdmin
09 — Audit
Audit Trail & Logs

Every system action is permanently recorded — who did it, what changed, and exactly when. Searchable and filterable by action type, exportable as CSV.

Super Admin

Issue Reporting

Operators submit issue reports — damaged clothes, lost items, or machine malfunctions. Reports route instantly to Admin's dashboard for review and resolution.
